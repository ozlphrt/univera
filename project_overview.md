# **Univera — College Guidance Made Simple**

## **1. Product Summary**
Univera is a mobile‑first, PWA‑based college counseling platform designed for high‑school students and their families. It simplifies the entire college search and admissions process through personalized recommendations, intelligent matching, structured guidance, and a beautifully intuitive interface inspired by Duolingo (fun, friendly, bite‑sized) and Airbnb (clean, premium, elegant).

The app acts as a centralized **College Admissions Command Center**, combining insights, planning tools, a student profile system, a task/timeline engine, and a powerful college match algorithm.

---

## **2. Vision Statement**
Empower every student and parent—regardless of background—to make confident, informed college decisions through clarity, personalization, and delightfully simple design.

---

## **3. Target Users**
### **Primary:**
- High school students (Grades 9–12)
- Parents supporting the college search

### **Secondary:**
- Counselors (future expansion)
- Educators & school systems (later phases)

---

## **4. Core Value Proposition**
Univera delivers:
- A personalized college list with reach/target/safety categories
- A dynamic admissions timeline with tasks and deadlines
- A structured student profile system
- A hybrid AI + rule‑based recommendation engine
- A clear overview of readiness, progress, and application strategy

All inside a **mobile‑first, PWA**, accessible anytime, anywhere.

---

## **5. Design Philosophy**
### **Duolingo influences:**
- Friendly micro‑interactions
- Bite‑sized progress
- Fun, adaptive onboarding

### **Airbnb influences:**
- Clean typography
- Visual clarity
- Soft cards, rounded corners, gentle shadows
- World‑class mobile UX

### **Tone:** Calm + Friendly with subtle personality.

---

## **6. High-Level Features**
1. **Smart Dashboard** — unified view of progress, tasks, profile, and college list
2. **College Match Engine** — dual-mode (simple sliders + advanced admissions logic)
3. **Student Profile** — academics, ECs, preferences, budget, personality
4. **College Database** — structured, rich profiles for each institution
5. **Timeline & Tasks** — month-by-month planning, smart reminders
6. **Essays Module** — brainstorming, prompts, story-building
7. **Parent View** — financial planning, deadlines, clarity
8. **AI Assistant** — Q&A guidance and smart suggestions
9. **Document Vault** — optional secure storage
10. **Advanced Mode** — counselor-level insights

---

## **7. Onboarding Flow**
- Full-screen card-based experience
- 10–12 question adaptive Quick Start
- Generates initial college list + dashboard
- Bottom navigation unlocks after onboarding

---

## **8. Navigation Structure**
### **Phase 1:** No nav bar — guided onboarding
### **Phase 2:** Bottom navigation bar
- Dashboard
- Colleges
- Tasks
- Profile
- More

---

## **9. Tech Principles**
- **Mobile-first PWA** (React 19 + Vite + TypeScript)
- **Offline-friendly** interactions
- **Modular, API-driven architecture**
- **Multi-user mode** (student + parent)
- **Data privacy principles built-in**

---

## **10. Long-Term Roadmap (High-Level)**
- Partnership integrations with external college databases
- Counselor dashboards
- Essay review marketplace
- Financial aid simulation tools
- AI narrative generator for essays & applications

---

## **11. Success Criteria**
- Clear college list within 5 minutes of onboarding
- +30% student clarity on reach/target/safety alignment
- High daily return rate via micro-tasks
- Parents understand financial & timeline expectations instantly
