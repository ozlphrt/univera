# **Univera — Intake Questions (Onboarding + Advanced Profile)**
This document contains the complete structured set of **student intake questions** used throughout Univera. The questions are grouped by purpose and usage stage.

The intake system must:
- Reduce friction (no long forms)
- Use simple, friendly language
- Support adaptive branching
- Capture enough data for meaningful college matches
- Avoid asking for unnecessary or sensitive information

---

# **1. Quick Start Onboarding (10–12 Questions)**
These appear during first-time onboarding and are designed to be answered in **under 2 minutes**.

## **1.1 Grade Level**
**Q:** *"What grade are you in?"*
- 9
- 10
- 11
- 12
- Gap year

## **1.2 Academic Self-Assessment**
**Q:** *"How would you describe your grades so far?"*
- Mostly A’s
- A’s and B’s
- Mostly B’s
- Below B’s
- Prefer not to say

Maps to synthetic GPA (for Simple Mode).

## **1.3 Intended Major**
**Q:** *"Do you have something you might want to study?"*
- Yes → (list of popular majors)
- Not sure yet → (skip)

## **1.4 Geographic Preference**
**Q:** *"Where would you love to study?"*
- Northeast
- South
- Midwest
- West
- West Coast
- Mountain West
- No preference

## **1.5 Campus Environment**
**Q:** *"What kind of campus do you imagine?"*
- Urban
- Suburban
- Small town / rural
- No preference

## **1.6 School Size Preference**
**Q:** *"What size school feels right to you?"*
- Small (under 5,000 students)
- Medium (5,000–15,000)
- Large (15,000+)
- No preference

## **1.7 Extracurricular Confidence**
**Q:** *"How strong do you feel your extracurriculars are?"*
- Slider 1–5

## **1.8 Academic Confidence**
**Q:** *"How confident do you feel about your academics?"*
- Slider 1–5

## **1.9 Cost Sensitivity**
**Q:** *"How important is cost for your family?"*
- Very important
- Somewhat important
- Not very important
- Prefer not to say

## **1.10 Application Timeline**
**Q:** *"Where are you in the application process?"*
- Just starting
- Starting soon
- Actively working on it
- Already applying

## **1.11 Bonus Adaptive Questions (Optional)**
Shown only when relevant.
- "Do you prefer a more collaborative or competitive academic vibe?"
- "Are sports or school spirit important to you?"

# **2. Advanced Profile Questions (Editable Anytime)**
These live in the Profile module. They expand the basic intake into a complete admissions profile.

---

# **2.1 Academics**

### **GPA & Coursework**
- *"What is your unweighted GPA?"* (0–4 scale)
- *"What is your weighted GPA?"* (optional)
- *"How would you describe your course rigor?"*
  - Low / Medium / High
- *"Have your grades improved over time?"*
  - Upward trend / Consistent / Declining

### **Test Scores**
- *"Have you taken any standardized tests?"*
  - SAT: ____
  - ACT: ____
- *"Do you have AP exam scores?"*
  - Add multiple entries

---

# **2.2 Extracurricular Activities**
**Each EC entry asks:**
- Activity title
- Category (sports, arts, work, leadership, STEM, service, etc.)
- Description (optional)
- Years involved
- Hours per week
- Leadership roles?
- Achievements?

---

# **2.3 Preferences**
- Preferred regions
- Preferred environments
- School size
- Campus vibe(s)
- Interest in Greek life? (optional)
- Importance of school prestige? (slider)
- Importance of diversity / inclusion? (optional)

---

# **2.4 Budget & Financial Aid**
- Annual family budget range
- Need financial aid? (yes/no)
- Open to out-of-state options? (yes/no)
- Open to public universities? (yes/no)

---

# **2.5 Personality & Learning Style**
- *"How do you learn best?"*
  - Visual / Hands-on / Reading & writing / Collaborative
- *"Which traits best describe you?"*
  - Motivated, curious, creative, analytical, empathetic, etc.
- *"Do you prefer structure or flexibility?"*
  - Structured / Flexible / Somewhere in between

# **4. Parent Intake Questions**
Used only if the user selects "I'm a parent".

## **4.1 Parent Role & Context**
- *"What is your relationship to the student?"* (Mother, Father, Guardian)
- *"What grade is your student in?"*

## **4.2 Financial Context**
- *"How important is managing cost for your family?"*
- *"Do you already have a sense of your budget?"* (optional)

## **4.3 Support Needs**
- *"What do you want your student to get from Univera?"*
  - Better list of schools
  - Stronger applications
  - Staying organized
  - Affordability guidance
  - All of the above

---

# **5. Adaptive Logic Summary**

### **5.1 Intended Major Branching**
If "I'm not sure": skip major competitiveness.
If major selected: advanced mode enables major fit scoring.

### **5.2 Financial Sensitivity Branching**
If "Very important": emphasize budget + merit aid schools.

### **5.3 Grade Level Branching**
If 12th grade: timeline adjusts to immediate deadlines.
If 9–11: timeline stretches over months.

### **5.4 EC/Academic Confidence Branching**
If low scores: suggestions are more supportive and profile completion prompts appear earlier.
